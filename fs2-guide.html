Flying a VOR: O'Hare to JFK

1. Tune NAV1 to 115.90 (the Kennedy VOR at JFK)

ToKeys
Choose NAV MHzCtrl+N, then 1 for NAV1 (2 for NAV2)
Choose NAV kHzCtrl+N, Ctrl+N quickly
Adjust. up, , down

2. Set the course (OBS) to 094

ToKeys
Choose the OBSCtrl+V, then 1 for NAV1 (2 for NAV2)
Turn the card. or ,, 2 degrees a press, until 094 is under the top mark

094 is the nearest card step to the straight line from O'Hare, 093.

3. Fly the needle

NAV1 showsDo
Needle centered, flag TOYou are on course
Needle left of centerSteer a little left
Needle right of centerSteer a little right

DME counts down your distance to the station. The station works only once the New York scenery loads: until then the needle and DME stay dead, so fly heading 093 (about 1,150 km, 620 nm).
