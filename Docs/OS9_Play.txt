NitrOS-9 Play
v12 Music Player by Roger Taylor
December 7, 2025

Play currently outputs to the best device on the host computer depending on the file format.
The F256K2 and Jr2 will use the SAM2695 MIDI chip and the dual SID chips, while
the Tandy Color Computer might use the Game Master Cartridge or 6-bit DAC for supported scores.

 8+ voice Lyra scores
 4-voice Musica II scores to the best
 Raw SID dump files

Examples of usage:
 play song1.mus song2.lyr song3.lyr
 play song4.lyr rock3.map song1.lyr song2.lyr
 play song1.mus song2.lyr song3.lyr
 play game.rsd
 play -z=playlist file
 play -z<playlist path
 play -z<playlist path&  (play in the background)
 play -z=playlist file&  (play in the background)

Simple 2-file playlist:
 frosty.mus
 winterw.lyr

An optional MIDI .map file is a list of 16 instrument codes separated by commas or spaces.
MIDI maps are applied to all future songs on the current command or playlist line.
Each new line in a play command or playlist clears mapping.

Example of someband.map:
 0,44,28,21,23,25,32,72,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,118

Example playlist where MIDI mapping is used:
 (The last line uses the map included in that file)

 xmas.map silverb.lyr letit.lyr
 piano.map ocome.lyr
 rock3.map lucidity.lyr dustwind.lyr
 jinglex3.lyr
